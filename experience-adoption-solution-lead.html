Going from Trainee to the first founding Adoption Manager at CatalystOne Engage

During my time at CatalystOne Engage, I worked closely with operations and the Customer Success team, with a strong focus on enabling the team to do their best work. The role was titled Adoption Solution Lead, but in practice it covered a much broader scope and was largely about building the foundations that allowed CS to operate effectively at scale.

A major part of my work was leading the migration of the entire Engage support function to CatalystOne’s central support organization in Norway, which sits within HCM. This included planning, execution, and hands-on coordination, as well as transferring product knowledge and ownership. In parallel, we built a completely new Help Center for Engage and designed the onboarding and training for the new support function, ensuring they could confidently handle Engage-specific cases from day one.

I also helped design how the support team actually works with tickets in practice. Together, we built a structured and scalable ticket workflow based on how we had learned to operate efficiently within Engage over the years, with clear ownership, escalation paths, and priorities.

In addition to support and operations, I was responsible for the CS CRM setup for Engage and was actively involved in the consolidation of the CS CRM systems between HCM and Engage. This involved both strategic decisions and hands-on work to align data, processes, and day-to-day usage across teams.

Overall, the role was very much about making things work behind the scenes, so that the Customer Success team could focus on delivering value to customers.
