From Ambiguity to Scale: My Journey as a Scaleup CEO Trainee at Populum

My journey as a Scaleup CEO Trainee started with a simple question from Johannes Midtbö, founder and CEO of Populum: Do you want to join me on this journey?

At the time, Populum was an HR Tech startup with around ten employees, strong momentum, and big ambitions. I joined as employee number ten and quickly became Johannes’ right-hand partner. What the role actually meant was not clearly defined. In hindsight, that ambiguity turned out to be one of the biggest strengths of the experience.

Looking back, my time at Populum roughly broke down into 25 percent go-to-market strategy, 25 percent sales, and 50 percent operations. This distribution was not planned. It was a direct consequence of scaling fast and needing to solve whatever problem was most urgent at the time.

During my period at Populum, the company grew from 10 MSEK in ARR to 40 MSEK. We scaled the team from 10 to 35 employees while maintaining an average eNPS above 90. What became clear to us as a company was that sustainable growth at speed only works if culture, clarity, and execution scale together. Numbers follow when people do.

On the commercial side, I worked closely with leadership on GTM strategy and had the opportunity to present concrete proposals and analyses directly to the management team. I learned how our ideal customer profile actually behaved in reality, not just on paper. Our ICP typically ranged from 500 to 5,000 employees, with an average deal size of around 500,000 SEK. Being involved in many of these deals helped me deeply understand buying processes, decision dynamics, and what truly drives trust in B2B HR Tech sales.

Sales was also where I developed quickly on a personal level. I pitched Populum at a large number of events and fairs, refining how to communicate value clearly, quickly, and confidently. Winning deals consistently taught us as a company that focus beats complexity, especially when selling to larger organizations.

Operationally, much of my work revolved around enabling scale. This included everything from internal processes and cross-team alignment to supporting hiring and organizational structure as the company grew. The key insight here was that operations are not about control, but about removing friction so teams can perform at their best.

One of the most important milestones during this journey was establishing a strong partnership with CatalystOne, a leading HCM platform. What started as a simple widget integration evolved into a deeper commercial collaboration where we began upselling each other’s products. Over time, this partnership proved both strategically and culturally strong, eventually leading to Populum becoming part of the CatalystOne group through an acquisition.

Joining a larger ecosystem marked a natural transition point. After completing my journey as a Scaleup CEO Trainee, I stepped into a new role as Adoption Solution Lead at CatalystOne. It felt like a continuation rather than an ending. The scaleup mindset, hands-on execution, and customer-centric thinking from Populum carried over directly into this next chapter.

In retrospect, the biggest takeaway from my time as a Scaleup CEO Trainee was not tied to a specific project or title. It was learning how fast-growing companies actually work when theory meets reality, and how impact is created by being close to both strategy and execution at the same time.
